      Ludo Night

      Race four pawns around the board and into the centre. Pick who plays each colour.

      How to play
      • Roll a 6 to bring a pawn out of your base.
      • A 6, a capture, or getting a pawn home gives you another roll.
      • Land on an opponent to send it back to base. Stars and start squares are safe.
      • Three 6s in a row and your turn is lost.
      • You need the exact number to reach the centre.
